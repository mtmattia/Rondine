<p>Rondine is an <b>open source</b> messenger. Messages are end-to-end encrypted with MLS (RFC 9420) and no central server can read them.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Usernames</b> in the form <i>@name@node</i>, like an email address.</li>
<li><b>Address book</b> discovery that only works between people who have each other's number. Numbers never leave the phone in clear text.</li>
<li><b>Invite links and QR codes</b>, revocable at any time.</li>
<li>Chats and <b>groups</b>, photos, documents, <b>voice messages</b>, live location.</li>
<li><b>Voice and video calls</b>, directly between phones and encrypted.</li>
<li>Small, replaceable <i>nodes</i> anyone can host; they only see encrypted data.</li>
<li>Notifications via UnifiedPush (e.g. ntfy), no Google services.</li>
</ul>
<p>Status: <b>alpha</b>. The app interface is in Italian for now.</p>
